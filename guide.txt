Feedback Project
---------------------
1. How did the evaluatee do on their project?
2. What did I do to check their code? If there was a mistake/ error, what was it?
3. What did we talk about?
4. Have I done this project before?
	If Yes:
	- Is there anything that can be improved?
	- What are some suggestions I can give?
	- Highlight interesting/ unique parts of the project.
	If No:
	- What did I learn?
	- What did I find interesting?
5. Summary of how did the evaluation go and my opinion of the evaluatee's understanding of the project.
6. Kind words.

Feedback Evaluation
---------------------
1. How was the evaluator? Were they friendly, rude or kind? First impressions.
2. How did the evaluation start and what did they do to check my code?
3. Has the evaluator done this project before?
	If Yes:
	- Were they rigorous in checking my code?
	- List out any cool insights they gave.
	If No:
	- What and how was their approach?
	- List out any suggestions I have.
4. How was the attitude of the evaluator? Was there anything I particularly enjoyed?
5. Summary of how the evaluation went and my opinion of how well the evaluator evaluted my project.
7. Grateful words.
